The name Gautam (or Gautama) is an ancient Sanskrit name meaning "the dispeller of darkness" or "one who illuminates." It is derived from the roots "go" (meaning light, knowledge, or star) and"tama" (meaningdarkness).Historically, it carries immense spiritual weight. It is most famously known as the clan name of Siddhartha Gautama, the spiritual teacher who founded Buddhism. In Hindu tradition, Gautam was also one of the Saptarishis (seven great sages).
